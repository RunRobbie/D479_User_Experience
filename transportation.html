Transportation

Getting to and around Taniti

Arriving by air

Almost all visitors arrive by air. Taniti's small airport currently handles small jets and propeller planes and is being expanded for larger jets.

Arriving by cruise ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night each week.

Getting around

Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City every day from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

Taxi & rental car

Taxis are available in Taniti City. Rental cars are available from a local agency near the airport.

Bike & walking

Bikes and helmets can be rented from several vendors. Helmets are required by law. Taniti City and Merriton Landing are both easy to explore on foot.
