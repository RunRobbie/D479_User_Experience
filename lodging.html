Places to stay

Taniti offers lodging for several budgets and travel styles. All lodging types are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

Budget hostel

An inexpensive option for travelers who want to keep lodging costs low.

Family-owned hotels

Many small, locally owned hotels are available across the island.

Four-star resort

Taniti has one large four-star resort for travelers seeking a more traditional resort experience.

Bed & breakfasts

A growing number of bed and breakfasts offer smaller-scale accommodations.

Other private lodging

Visitors may also encounter private-home or condo-style stays when planning their trip.
