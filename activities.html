Things to Do

Activities and sightseeing

Many visitors come for Taniti's beaches, rainforest, and volcano, with additional activities concentrated around Taniti City and Merriton Landing.

Beaches

Spend time on the white sandy beaches surrounding Yellow Leaf Bay or explore the island's rocky shoreline.

Rainforest adventures

Hike through the rainforest or try zip-lining.

Active volcano

Visit Taniti's small active volcano in the mountainous interior.

Water activities

Choose from snorkeling and chartered fishing tours.

Merriton Landing

Visit pubs, a microbrewery, a new dance club, and other entertainment in this rapidly developing area north of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Culture and entertainment

Visit the local history museum, art galleries, a movie theater, arcade, or bowling venue.

Other options include helicopter rides, boat and bus tours, and a nine-hole golf course expected to open next year.
