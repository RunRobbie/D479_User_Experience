Travel Info

Practical information

CurrencyU.S. dollars are used. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and major credit cards are widely accepted.
PowerPower outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States.
LanguageMany younger Tanitians speak fluent English. English is less common in rural areas, especially among older residents.
Health careTaniti has one hospital and several clinics. The hospital has many multilingual employees.
AlcoholThe drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be served or sold between midnight and 9 a.m.
SafetyViolent crime is very rare, although reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes have increased with tourism.
National holidaysMany attractions and restaurants close on national holidays, so visitors should plan ahead.
GroceriesTaniti has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one convenience store open 24 hours.
